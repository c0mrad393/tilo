27 November 2026

Tilo has closed

Tilo was a studio in the browser for drawing by hand, where every piece kept its replay, stroke by stroke. Thank you to everyone who drew on it.

The site and its server are gone, and the accounts, emails and pieces they held have been deleted. The code lives on, open source under the MIT license, for anyone who wants to run it or build on it.
