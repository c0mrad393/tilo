Why Tilo

Three things a portfolio site can't do

proof of hand

Every piece has its replay

Pieces are drawn on Tilo, so each one carries the full stroke record. Anyone can scrub through it. That shows the work is human-made without anyone having to argue about it.

limits help

Prompts with rules

One subject, six colours, ten minutes. A beginner and a professional start from the same sheet, so comparing their work is fair.

pass it on

Remix chains

Pick up someone's piece and keep drawing on top. Credit stays attached and every piece shows where it came from and what grew out of it.
